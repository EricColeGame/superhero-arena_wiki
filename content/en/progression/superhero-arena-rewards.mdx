## What the Official Page Actually Documents

That is essentially the whole documented progression surface. The page also lists the experience as Action / Battlegrounds & Fighting, caps servers at 15 players, and notes access through Roblox on Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Xbox, and PlayStation.

Two dates appear on the listing: the page was created on September 6, 2026, and updated on October 29, 2026. An update timestamp is not a patch note, so no reward changes can be confirmed from it.

## The One Confirmed Reward: Unlocking New Heroes

In the verified material, the reward-like outcome is hero access. The description states plainly that defeating players unlocks new heroes, and that Thanos is the warlord you work toward.

What the page does not state matters just as much. There is no documented currency, no cost per hero, no unlock order, no threshold, and no progress meter. So the safest way to describe Superhero Arena rewards is this: you play matches, you defeat opponents, and hero unlocks are the documented result. Anything beyond that is outside the evidence.
